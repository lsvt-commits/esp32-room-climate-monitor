Relative humidity

Humidity guidance

    The reading is relative humidity: water vapor relative to saturation at the current temperature. A reading of 50% means half that limit; it does not mean half the air is water.

    General indoor guidance uses 30–50% as a reference, staying below 60%. The appropriate range depends on the room and season.

    Humidity ranges
    HumidityInterpretation
    Below 30%Dry air.
    30–50%Indoor reference range.
    Above 50%, below 60%Above the reference range; check whether it persists.
    60% or higherPersistent high humidity can contribute to condensation and mold, especially on cold surfaces.

    Use trends over hours and days. A brief peak does not describe the whole room. The DHT11 has limited accuracy; small differences near thresholds are not an alarm.

    Source: EPA · indoor humidity.

    Temperature guidance

      The sensor measures air at its location. Keep it away from radiators, direct sunlight, windows and the ESP32’s heat. The DHT11 has limited accuracy; focus on trends rather than small decimal changes.

      Cold-weather reference: UKHSA · indoor heating. Displayed comfort thresholds are indicative.
